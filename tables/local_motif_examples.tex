Notation: \texttt{S}=source, \texttt{T}=target, \texttt{B}=node present in both reference and prediction, \texttt{E}=node present only in the reference, \texttt{P}=node present only in the prediction. The motifs condense recurring local graph signatures rather than individual examples.}
    \label{tab:local-motif-examples}
    \small
    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{p{0.20\textwidth}p{0.25\textwidth}X}
        \toprule
        Failure family & Example local motif & Interpretation \\
        \midrule
        Shorter trace & \texttt{S -> B -> B; B -> E -> ...} & The prediction follows a valid prefix and then stops before the reference suffix. \\
        Over-exploration & \texttt{S -> B -> P -> P} & The model continues traversing prediction-only nodes after leaving the reference path. \\
        Alternative branch & \texttt{B -> E; B -> P} & The prediction shares a prefix, then chooses a sibling or nearby branch instead of the oracle branch. \\
        Invalid transition & \texttt{B -> P} without a graph edge & The predicted transition is not a valid local call edge. \\
        Missed reachable target & \texttt{S -> B -> E -> T} & The target is reachable in the executable graph, but the predicted path omits the suffix containing the target. \\
        Longer trace & \texttt{S -> B -> T -> P} & The prediction reaches the relevant region but continues beyond the reference trace. \\
        Invalid introduction & \texttt{S -> B1, S -> B2} & A node is introduced before any earlier predicted node has an executable edge to it. \\
        Empty prediction & \texttt{S -> E, S -> E} & The reference contains reachable outgoing structure, but the extracted prediction is empty. \\
        Hallucinated target & \texttt{B -> P -> T} & The prediction reaches the target through a prediction-only suffix absent from the oracle path. \\
        Wrong-rank target & \texttt{S -> T -> B} vs. \texttt{S -> B -> T} & The target appears, but at the wrong position in the traversal. \\
        Repeated methods & \texttt{B -> P -> P} & A method is repeated even though path correctness requires a duplicate-free sequence. \\
        Unresolved node & \texttt{B -> P(unresolved)} & The prediction introduces a method name that cannot be resolved in the parsed call graph. \\
        Wrong source & \texttt{P -> P; S -> E} & The predicted trace starts from a node other than the required source anchor. \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabularx}
