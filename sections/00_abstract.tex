Large language models are increasingly evaluated on realistic software-engineering tasks, but broad end-to-end benchmarks can conflate many distinct capabilities. We introduce \benchmark, a controlled Java benchmark for evaluating whether LLMs can answer and explain method-reachability questions under exact oracle supervision. \benchmark focuses on tree-shaped call graphs generated from randomized pseudo-binary trees and studies two task families: control-flow reachability, where generated method calls are unguarded, and condition-aware reachability, where visible calls may be placed behind conditions whose truth values determine executable reachability. The benchmark separates final-answer correctness from canonical execution-trace accuracy, trace completeness, graph-valid exploration, and invalid-output behavior.
