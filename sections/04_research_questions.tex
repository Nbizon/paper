\section{Research Questions}
\label{sec:research-questions}

We organize our evaluation around three research questions. The first studies whether models answer reachability questions correctly and how controlled factors affect their performance. The second compares the different model and inference configurations. The third analyzes the traces returned by the models to understand the errors that are not visible from the final answer alone.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \researchq{1}: How accurately do LLMs solve executable method-reachability questions, and how is their performance affected by canonical trace length, class size, query polarity, and condition-aware guards?
    \item \researchq{2}: How do model and inference configurations compare on \benchmark, and how stable are the improvements obtained with verified reasoning controls?
    \item \researchq{3}: What information do model-generated traces provide about answer--trace consistency, graph-valid exploration, recurring errors, and syntactic-call bias?
\end{itemize}
