\section{Research Questions}
\label{sec:research-questions}

The objective of the evaluation is not only to rank the models. We also want to identify which properties of a reachability question make it difficult and determine whether the returned trace supports the final answer. We therefore study three research questions.

\noindent\researchq{1}: \emph{How accurately do LLMs solve executable method-reachability questions, and how is their performance affected by trace length, class size, query polarity, and condition-aware guards?}

This first question establishes the general performance of the models and relates their errors to the factors controlled by the generator.

\noindent\researchq{2}: \emph{How do model and inference configurations compare on \benchmark, and how stable are the improvements obtained with verified reasoning controls?}

This question determines whether the observed differences come from the evaluated model configuration and whether increasing the reasoning budget continues to improve the answer and the trace.

\noindent\researchq{3}: \emph{What information do model-generated traces provide about the errors made by LLMs?}

The last question compares each trace with the oracle graph. It studies whether the trace is consistent with the final answer, whether it follows executable calls, and which error patterns occur repeatedly.
