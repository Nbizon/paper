\section{Research Questions}
\label{sec:research-questions}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \researchq{1}: How accurately do LLMs solve executable method-reachability queries, and how do structural factors such as canonical execution distance, class size, and condition-aware guards affect final-answer and trace-level performance?
    \item \researchq{2}: Which models are most effective on ReachabilityBench, and do reasoning models yield robust improvements over standard non-reasoning models ?
    \item \researchq{3}: What do model-generated traces reveal about LLM reachability reasoning, including answer--trace consistency, graph-valid exploration, recurrent error patterns, and syntactic-call bias?
\end{itemize}
