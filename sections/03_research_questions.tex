\section{Research Questions}
\label{sec:research-questions}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \researchq{1}: How accurately do LLMs solve method-reachability queries, and how do structural factors in the code affect their performance?
    \item \researchq{2}: What do the generated traces reveal about LLM reasoning on reachability tasks?
    \item \researchq{3}: Is there a relationship between model performance on ReachabilityBench and performance on existing benchmarks?
\end{itemize}
