\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presents \benchmark, the final generated dataset of Java classes and executable method-reachability questions, together with its configurable construction algorithm. Controlled factors and execution-validated labels and graphs allow us to examine reachability decisions and supporting exploration traces separately.

The evaluation shows that correct decisions can accompany incomplete or non-executable evidence. The strongest difficulties involve guarded calls, passed values, and the exhaustive exploration required by negative questions. The CO/EE comparison shows how the same dataset can expose different behavior when its execution context is made explicit. The gains remain modest for Gemma and Qwen3.6; Qwen3.8-Flash-Next improves its traces more on the difficult subset, with more non-responses.

Evaluating decisions and traces separately makes it possible to locate difficulties that final-answer correctness alone leaves unresolved. Further evaluation on more models and real programs would test how broadly these difficulties transfer.
