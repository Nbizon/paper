\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\benchmark is a controlled Java benchmark for evaluating whether LLMs can answer and explain method-reachability questions under exact oracle supervision. The benchmark is designed as a diagnostic complement to realistic software-engineering benchmarks: it isolates an elementary structural capability, separates final-answer correctness from trace quality, and supports controlled variation across task families and difficulty factors.

The final ICSE study will compare control-flow and condition-aware reachability, quantify degradation introduced by guarded calls and condition evaluation, and analyze requested reasoning-budget effects for reasoning models.
