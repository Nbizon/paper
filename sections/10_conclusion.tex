\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presents \benchmark, a controlled benchmark for studying how LLMs reason about Java control-flow reachability under explicit requested reasoning budgets. The completed control-flow study shows that budget can help, but its value depends strongly on the model and on the metric being optimized. Qwen converts budget into large gains in both final answers and chain fidelity, Gemma saturates early, and GPT-OSS improves final answers more than traces. Across all models, final-answer accuracy substantially overstates path-level reasoning quality.
