\subsection{\researchq{2}: Task Family and Structural Factors}

RQ2 analyzes the effect of task family and structural difficulty. We report absolute performance for each task family, but focus the comparative analysis on the degradation from control-flow reachability to condition-aware reachability. Because both families share the same reachability question, output schema, and trace-based evaluation protocol, the performance drop isolates the additional difficulty introduced by guarded calls and condition evaluation.

The analysis distinguishes graph-structural factors from code-surface factors. Graph-structural factors include signed canonical distance, tree depth, tree shape, branching, source-target position, and positive versus negative queries. Code-surface factors include context size, comments, local variables, loops, \texttt{if} statements, parameters, and method order shuffling. The tree-expansion parameter $p$ is fixed and is not treated as a main experimental factor.

\subsection{\researchq{3}: Reasoning Budget Analysis}

RQ3 is limited to reasoning models that expose requested reasoning-budget control. For control-flow reachability, we analyze budgets from 512 to 16,384 requested reasoning tokens and report the smallest requested reasoning budget needed to reach at least 95\% of the maximum observed final-answer accuracy for each reasoning model. We also report \texttt{NO\_RESPONSE} rate across budgets.

Budget sensitivity is secondary to the main benchmark comparison. We do not make reasoning versus non-reasoning the central story, and we do not claim that equal total generation allowance implies equal compute cost or equal internal allocation.
