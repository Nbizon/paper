\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

This section reports the condition-aware experiment after rescoring every response against the executable graph. Each model is evaluated on the same 46,656 questions, and all percentages include unparsable responses. The audit found no disagreement between the 46,656 labels and executable-graph reachability. By contrast, the former syntactic reconstruction made the target appear reachable in 13,273 of 23,328 negative questions (56.90\%), explaining the previous artificial ceiling on negative path accuracy.

\subsection{\researchq{1}: Reachability Accuracy and Structural Factors}

Table~\ref{tab:overall} reports the two independent primary metrics. Qwen obtains the strongest result for final-answer correctness (87.98\%) and path correctness (67.43\%). Gemma reaches 84.21\% and 55.90\%, respectively. Qwen's advantage is 3.77 percentage points for the Boolean decision and 11.53 points for the evidence path. Formatting failure is more frequent for Gemma (2.24\%) than for Qwen (0.83\%).

\input{tables/overall\_performance}

Distance affects path correctness more strongly than final answers (Table~\ref{tab:distance}). From distance 1 to 25, Gemma's final-answer accuracy falls by 12.54 points and its path accuracy by 36.50 points. Qwen loses 8.20 and 25.95 points, respectively. At distance 25, Boolean accuracy remains at least 80\% for both models, while path accuracy is 43.58\% for Gemma and 57.23\% for Qwen.

\input{tables/distance\_performance}

The marginal contrasts in Table~\ref{tab:factors} identify guards and parameters as the strongest generated factors. Moving from zero to five guards reduces path accuracy by 44.78 points for Gemma and 47.73 points for Qwen. Moving from zero to two parameter levels reduces it by 23.83 and 38.50 points. Comments, local variables, and loops have smaller marginal associations.

Class size affects the models differently. Moving from 100 to 500 methods reduces Gemma's final accuracy from 86.81\% to 80.67\% and its path accuracy from 64.83\% to 45.45\%. Qwen's final accuracy rises slightly from 86.83\% to 88.85\%, while its path accuracy falls from 69.55\% to 64.93\%.

\input{tables/factor\_effects}

\paragraph{Answer to \researchq{1}.}
Both models solve most condition-aware reachability decisions, with Qwen reaching 87.98\% final-answer accuracy. Path correctness is lower and degrades more sharply with distance, guards, parameters, and class size.

\subsection{\researchq{2}: Evidence from Generated Paths}

Table~\ref{tab:trace-diagnostics} separates reachable and unreachable questions. On reachable queries, Qwen obtains 94.08\% final-answer correctness and 85.72\% path correctness; Gemma reaches 85.19\% and 73.96\%. For unreachable queries, path correctness falls to 49.15\% for Qwen and 37.84\% for Gemma. A positive path may stop at the target, whereas a negative path must enumerate the entire source-reachable closure and exclude the target.

\input{tables/trace\_diagnostics}

The corrected negative scores are no longer limited by the former 43.1\% artificial ceiling because path validity ignores calls merely present in source text. Executable-graph scoring also avoids enforcing one deterministic depth-first serialization: alternative orders receive the same score when each method is introduced through an executable edge from an earlier presented method and the polarity-specific endpoint or coverage rule holds. Consequently, \texttt{correctness\_path} measures possible executable evidence rather than imitation of a generated ordering.

\paragraph{Answer to \researchq{2}.}
The paths reveal a substantial answer--evidence gap, especially for exhaustive proofs of unreachability. Executable-graph scoring distinguishes valid alternative explorations from disconnected, incomplete, or non-executable traces without coupling this judgment to the Boolean answer.

\subsection{\researchq{3}: Relation to Existing Benchmarks}

The present artifact contains two model configurations evaluated on the generated condition-aware benchmark, but no paired scores for these exact configurations on an external code-reasoning benchmark. A model-level correlation would in any case be uninformative with only two observations. We therefore do not report a correlation or claim that the ranking observed here transfers to existing benchmarks.

\paragraph{Answer to \researchq{3}.}
The available measurements establish performance on controlled method reachability only; they do not provide evidence for a relationship with external benchmark performance.
