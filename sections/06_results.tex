\subsection{\researchq{2}: Models and Inference Modes}

RQ2 compares model configurations as deployed, including intended inference mode and whether the serving stack exposes verified reasoning-budget controls. The analysis avoids treating model family names alone as the unit of comparison: the same base model may behave differently under different serving modes, output budgets, or reasoning controls.

For configurations with verified requested reasoning-budget controls, we analyze budgets from 512 to 16,384 requested reasoning tokens and report the smallest budget that reaches at least 95\% of the model's maximum observed final-answer accuracy. We also track trace metrics and \texttt{NO\_RESPONSE} across budgets. Budget sensitivity is not used to make claims about configurations whose serving stack does not expose a verified control.
