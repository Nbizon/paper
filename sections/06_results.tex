\subsection{\researchq{2}: Models and Inference Configurations}

The second analysis compares the complete configurations listed in Table~\ref{tab:models}. A checkpoint can behave differently depending on its serving mode, context limit, output budget, or reasoning control. For this reason, the results are reported for configurations and not only for model names.

For configurations with a verified reasoning-budget control, we test requested budgets from 512 to 16,384 tokens. We report the smallest budget that reaches at least 95\% of the highest final-answer accuracy observed for that configuration. We then check whether trace quality and the \texttt{NO\_RESPONSE} rate follow the same evolution. We do not report conclusions about reasoning budgets for deployments where the control has not been confirmed.
